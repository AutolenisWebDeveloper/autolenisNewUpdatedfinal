# AutoLenis — Platform-Wide Gap Analysis

**Scope:** the entire application — buyer journey, transactional/financial core (auctions→offers→deals→deposits→e-sign→pickup), admin console (138 pages), affiliate/growth/public marketing, and cross-cutting reliability (47 crons, 16 QStash jobs, 7 webhooks, all integrations) plus architecture/tech-debt.

**Method:** six parallel deep read-only audits, then consolidation + de-duplication of cross-agent overlaps. High-severity claims were **spot-verified against the source** — one agent's top claim ("CI is broken / no test gate") was **false** (the real `ci.yml` uses pnpm and runs `pnpm test`) and has been dropped; the five most severe findings below were each confirmed by direct grep/read. Nothing was modified during discovery.

**Verdict.** The foundations are genuinely strong: Stripe *deposit capture* is transactionally idempotent, auction close/activation self-heal via reconcilers, RBAC/tenancy are solid (no cross-dealer IDOR), TypeScript is strict (3 `any`, 0 `@ts-ignore`), and CI runs typecheck+lint+test+build. **The gaps are not missing systems — they are (1) unwired completions where the backend exists but nothing calls it, (2) unhandled unhappy-paths at the highest-stakes moments (contract/e-sign/insurance/refund), and (3) an idempotency/observability discipline that exists in a few reference spots but isn't the platform default.** Money-in is safe; money-out, contract verification, and the "what happens when it goes wrong" surfaces are where state gets stuck or inconsistent.

---

## The 8 recurring themes (programs of work, not scattered tickets)

**A. Idempotency & at-least-once safety is islanded, not the default.** Stripe refunds (no `idempotencyKey`), deposit PIs, all 16 QStash job sends, Twilio inbound, and several webhooks can double-execute or vanish on normal retries. The correct pattern already exists (`service-fee.service`, `auction-close` cron, `social-publish-queue`, DocuSign dealer-marketplace, MicroBilt) — it needs to become universal.

**B. The contract → e-sign → insurance completion cluster is the single most serious area.** Contract Shield scans placeholder text and auto-approves; declined/voided/expired DocuSign envelopes are unhandled and strand deals with money already captured; insurance bind/verify record policies with no provider call; and the buyer can never download their executed contract. This one cluster contains 4+ criticals and was independently flagged by three agents.

**C. "Backend built, UI unwired" — the highest-leverage fixes.** Whole capabilities have a working API/service with zero callers: affiliate onboarding review, CRM campaign kill-switch (`setStatus`), SEO keyword CRUD, insurance `bindPolicy`, referral milestones, saved searches. The hard part is done; these are wiring jobs.

**D. Broken outbound links & dead-end funnels.** Pickup-complete email, payment-prefs email, refinance SMS, and the deal-complete referral link all 404 or are empty; the sitemap advertises `/cars/*` pages that `notFound()`; the warmest lead traffic is dumped on the form-less `/request-a-car` instead of the real `/request-vehicle` form.

**E. Observability is wired to nobody.** The `cron-monitor` service is called by **0 of 47 crons**; health alerts write to the same DB that may be down; two nurture crons (`lead-magnet-sequence`, `social-lead-nurture`) aren't scheduled at all; and ~8 capabilities silently no-op when an env var is unset. A dead cron or a poisoned email key is invisible until a human opens the admin console.

**F. Cron/webhook auth is single-layer and can fail open.** Every cron accepts the spoofable `x-vercel-cron: 1` header alone, and the `Bearer` comparison becomes the literal `"Bearer undefined"` when `CRON_SECRET` is unset — a misconfiguration makes side-effecting crons world-triggerable. (Systemic; 44 routes.)

**G. Compliance edges.** The email idempotency bug can permanently kill the **FCRA §615 adverse-action notice**; MicroBilt-declined applicants are told **"APPROVED"**; the **$99 "Refundable" vs "non-refundable"** contradiction sits on the primary paid-traffic LP (flagged by 3 agents); onboarding **TCPA SMS consent** is collected but never persisted.

**H. Architecture/performance debt.** Missing Prisma `@@index` on `Offer`/`Deal`/`Auction` FK+status columns; 188 unbounded `findMany`; dead deps (`axios`, `ts-node`) and dead CRA scaffolding (`craco.config.js`, 46 unused `src/components/ui/*.jsx`); no code-splitting; ~363 API routes without zod; stringly-typed enums.

### Architecture / tech-debt / upgrades
- **Performance:** add Prisma `@@index` on `Offer.{auctionId,dealerId,status}`, `Deal.{buyerId,status}`, `Auction.{buyerId,status}` + child tables (`schema.prisma` ~399-620); bound the **188** unbounded `findMany` (admin lists + report aggregations first); code-split `recharts`/`leaflet` and the 1,600–4,700-line client god-files with `next/dynamic`; migrate 16 raw `<img>`→`next/image`; scope `next.config.mjs images.remotePatterns` off the `"**"` wildcard.
- **Dead weight:** delete `craco.config.js`, `jsconfig.json`, `plugins/health-check/*`, 46 unused `src/components/ui/*.jsx`; drop `axios` + `ts-node` deps (0 imports each). *(Verified.)*
- **Consistency:** standardize a `validate(schema)` zod wrapper (only 171/534 routes validate); promote stringly-typed statuses (`ContractScan`, `Vehicle.condition`, `AuditLog.action`, `AffiliatePayout.*`, `HealthCheck.status`) to Prisma enums.
- **Upgrades:** Prisma 5→6 (query engine + serverless pooling); adopt Server Actions for admin mutation forms (only 1 `"use server"` file vs 534 routes); bump `engines.node` to `>=20.9`.
- **Testing:** CI *does* run `pnpm test` (the "broken CI" claim was false); gaps are auth (MFA lockout/rate-limit) and one e2e on buyer→deposit→auction→deal.
