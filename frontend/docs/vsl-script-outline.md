# AutoLenis VSL Production Brief

> Total runtime: **75–95 seconds.** Master version targets 85s. Channel cutdowns
> derive from this master. Subtitles burned-in at all times. No quantitative
> savings claims (e.g. "average $X saved") until the AutoLenis savings
> methodology document is published; until then, language is qualitative
> ("thousands reported in savings", "negotiation leverage"). Every UI shown on
> screen is the real shipped buyer dashboard — no mockups, no placeholder data
> that does not reflect actual behaviour. Branded outro card stays on screen a
> minimum of **1.5s** so the AutoLenis logo is unambiguous in muted scrolling
> feed playback.

### Production rules (non-negotiable)
- Subtitles burned-in. 88% of paid social plays on mute.
- No specific dollar-amount savings claims until the AutoLenis savings methodology
  document is published. Qualitative wording only.
- UI captures must use the live shipped product. No marketing mockups.
- Anonymized dealer tags must read "Anonymized Dealer A/B/C" — never a real
  dealer name unless written consent is on file.
- Outro card must hold ≥ 1.5s with the AutoLenis wordmark and the URL only.
- Master audio mix: −16 LUFS for platform compliance.
- Aspect deliverables: 9:16 (1080×1920 — TikTok / Reels / Shorts), 1:1
  (1080×1080 — Facebook in-feed), 16:9 (1920×1080 — YouTube pre-roll).

## Channel variant cutdowns

Each variant lifts the strongest beat for that channel to the front. Master
script remains the source of truth — variants only re-order or trim.

### Facebook — Identity lead
- 0–4s · Buyer at home, calm. Hook: "You are not the person on the showroom floor."
- 5–35s · Mechanism + product proof.
- 36–45s · CTA.

### TikTok — Hook lead
- 0–3s · Salesperson approaching. Cut. Buyer on couch. Subtitle: "The table just turned."
- 4–25s · Fast UI cuts: request → auction → ranked offers.
- 26–30s · CTA card.

### YouTube — Mechanism lead
- 0–8s · Screen recording, full mechanism walkthrough.
- 9–55s · Product proof + trust anchors.
- 56–75s · Emotional payoff + CTA.

### Google (display + YouTube bumper) — Outcome lead
- 0–4s · Ranked offers card centered. Subtitle: "Three dealers competed for me."
- 5–14s · Trust anchors + URL.
- 15–20s · CTA card.
