PART A

Where every form goes

The transaction does not begin at account creation. It begins on a marketing page, a search result, a social post, or an affiliate link. Every form on the website, without exception, resolves to exactly one of four lanes — and every buy-lane form lands on the same fulfillment record.

PART C

The money model

Exactly three money movements exist in an AutoLenis transaction, and only the first two touch AutoLenis. Stripe is the authority on money; local state is never treated as authoritative over the provider.

PART C

Inventory and the shortlist

The catalogue is a purchased third-party feed swept into the platform on a schedule, not dealer stock committed to AutoLenis and not searched live per buyer. That makes a listing a specification rather than a supply promise. After prequalification the system automatically presents qualifying vehicles within 100 miles of the buyer’s ZIP and inside their approved ceiling — but it never chooses for them. The 100-mile ceiling is AutoLenis policy, not a vendor limit: a provider must support it, and no change to a provider plan may move it. Sourcing is unaffected and still reaches 250 miles through the rooftop pool.

Four experiences, and who acts in each

The system finds vehicles. The buyer chooses them. Auto-saving a recommendation into a shortlist would imply intent the buyer never expressed, and could carry an unwanted vehicle into a paid auction.

The catalogue rules

A shortlisted vehicle is a candidate. One request carries up to five, the auction covers all of them, offers bind to the candidate they answer, and selection collapses the shortlist to a single VIN. Nothing after selection knows the request was ever multi-vehicle.

PART C

Plans, upgrades and downgrades

There are two plans and one gate. Standard is not a free tier — the $99 paid at the gate is the Standard plan in full. Premium is $499 in total, collected as that same $99 plus a $400 balance. The upgrade window opens when the $99 settles and closes when funding clears. It is pitched hardest immediately after the buyer accepts an offer — they have just chosen their car, and everything Premium does still lies ahead of them. Moving between plans never changes a single transaction term.

The upgrade sequence

Premium is the second revenue line, so the ask is deliberate and sequenced rather than left to a link in a menu. Five touchpoints, one of which does most of the work. Then it stops.

PART C

Financing happens outside AutoLenis

Whether the dealership arranges it, the buyer brings it, or AutoLenis assists in finding it, the loan is obtained and completed with an external lender. AutoLenis refers, coordinates, follows up, and verifies — and does nothing else. It never collects an SSN, accepts a lender application, pulls lender credit, underwrites, issues lender disclosures, or disburses funds.

Operational evidence gathered against the live system, recorded here so the remediation sequence can be justified without embedding dated observations in the permanent rules. These are findings, not business rules, and they expire once the remediation lands.

The permanent rules these findings justify are simply: synchronise inventory to served markets; monitor provider usage; alert on quota thresholds; detect incomplete or abnormally small synchronisation results; and never show a buyer a false no-inventory result caused by a provider failure.
